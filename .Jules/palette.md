## 2025-02-28 - Add missing aria-label and focus-visible states to Navigation
**Learning:** The application's component library has a historical pattern of missing `aria-label` attributes and `focus-visible` keyboard focus states on icon-only buttons, as seen in the Navbar.
**Action:** Future UX work should proactively verify and add `aria-label` and `focus-visible` states to interactive elements, particularly icon-only buttons, to ensure keyboard navigation and screen reader accessibility.
