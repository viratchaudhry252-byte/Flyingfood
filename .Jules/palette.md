## 2024-11-05 - Missing Focus States and ARIA Labels on Icon-Only Buttons\n**Learning:** The application's component library has a historical pattern of missing `aria-label` attributes and `focus-visible` keyboard focus states on icon-only buttons (e.g., in Navbar, ShoppingCart, CategoryNav, and MenuGrid).\n**Action:** Future UX work should proactively verify and add `aria-label` attributes and `focus-visible` classes to all interactive elements, especially icon-only buttons, to ensure accessibility and keyboard navigation support.
