## 2024-05-14 - Icon-Only Button Accessibility Pattern
**Learning:** Historical pattern across app components reveals icon-only buttons often lack `aria-label` and `focus-visible` keyboard focus states.
**Action:** Always proactively verify and add descriptive `aria-label` attributes and keyboard focus rings (e.g., `focus-visible:outline-none focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-[#ff4e00] rounded-full`) to icon-only buttons in future UX updates.
